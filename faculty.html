Principal & Academic Head

Dr. Neelam Mishra

B.A., M.A. (Sociology & Home Science), Ph.D.

Dr. Neelam Mishra is a respected academician and capable administrator. She holds a B.A., an M.A. in Sociology and Home Science, and a Ph.D. She began her career as an Assistant Professor in 2006 and became Principal on 30 April 2024. Committed to women’s empowerment and social welfare, she leads initiatives promoting education and self-reliance among women. Her visionary leadership, administrative excellence, and communication skills have raised the institution’s standards of academic quality and social responsibility.

Eternal Inspiration

The Visionary Founder: Pandit Shyam Lal Tiwari

M.A. in Defence Studies (Lucknow University) • Social Reformer & Thinker

Pandit Shyam Lal Tiwari dedicated his life to the upliftment of society and nation-building. Inspired by his revered mother Smt. Chhiviraji Devi, he founded Sarvodaya Sewa Sansthan to bring higher education to Jagdishpur, ensuring that regional students, especially from rural backgrounds, have access to university education.

His tireless efforts culminated in the laying of the foundation stone on 27 April 2001 by Smt. Sonia Gandhi. Today, his vision continues to guide our academic policies, discipline, and competitive examination coaching cell.

How to Enroll

Simple Admission Procedure

Straightforward admission process designed to assist students and parents from Jagdishpur and surrounding regions.

1. Application Submission

Obtain the admission registration form from the college office on Shukul Bazar Road, Jagdishpur, or submit an inquiry online.

2. Document Verification

Submit attested photocopies of High School (10th) & Intermediate (12th) marksheets, Transfer Certificate (TC), and Aadhaar Card.

3. Subject Counseling

Consult with our academic advisors to select optimal subject combinations in Bachelor of Arts or choose your M.A. / UPRTOU program.

4. Enrollment Confirmation

Complete fee deposition and receive your college identity card and syllabus booklet to begin your academic semester.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Details about admission, fees, university affiliation, and open university programs.

Which university is Rajiv Gandhi Mahavidyalaya affiliated with?

Rajiv Gandhi Mahavidyalaya is permanently affiliated to Dr. Ram Manohar Lohia Awadh University, Ayodhya. Degrees awarded are fully recognized nationwide.

What is the U.P. Rajarshi Tandon Open University (UPRTOU) Study Center?

The college hosts an official study center of UPRTOU, allowing local students, working individuals, and women to pursue various UG and PG degrees, diplomas, and certificates through flexible distance learning without travelling outside Jagdishpur.

Is competitive examination coaching available for B.A. students?

Yes. As per the founding resolution of Pt. Shyam Lal Tiwari, coaching for central and state-level competitive examinations (UPPCS, SSC, Railway, Banking, Teaching) is integrated within the Arts faculty.

Who can I contact for admission inquiries?

You can directly contact Dr. Neelam Mishra, Principal (+91 91203 74882) or Shri Arun Kumar, CEO (+91 90266 74882), or visit the campus office on Shukul Bazar Road, Jagdishpur, Amethi.

Our Legacy & Heritage

Founded with a Vision to Place Jagdishpur on the Educational Map

"Education has been the driving force behind human progress, from the earliest civilizations to the present scientific age."

The Jagdishpur area of Amethi district was placed on the industrial map through the sincere efforts of the late Shri Rajiv Gandhi, a beloved leader of the nation's youth. Yet the region's educational development remained incomplete. Pt. Shyam Lal Tiwari, a hardworking and visionary son of Jagdishpur, felt this gap deeply.

Inspired by his revered mother, Smt. Chhiviraji Devi, he resolved to establish a college for higher education in Jagdishpur. To realize this vision, he founded the Sarvodaya Sewa Sansthan, Jagdishpur, and with the support of his associates named the college after Shri Rajiv Gandhi. Teaching began in 1998 on a self-financed basis, affiliated to Dr. Ram Manohar Lohia Awadh University, Ayodhya.

Foundation Stone (27 April 2001): Laid by Smt. Sonia Gandhi, then President of Indian National Congress and MP from Amethi.
Permanent Affiliation: Received permanent affiliation from Dr. Ram Manohar Lohia Awadh University in 2003.
Competitive Exam Focus: Integrated coaching for central and state-level competitive examinations organized within the Arts faculty.

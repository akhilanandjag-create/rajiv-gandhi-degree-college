Our Story

The Jagdishpur area of Amethi district was placed on the industrial map through the sincere efforts of the late Shri Rajiv Gandhi, a beloved leader of the nation’s youth. Yet the region’s educational development remained incomplete.

Pt. Shyam Lal Tiwari, a hardworking and visionary son of Jagdishpur, felt this gap deeply. To place the region on the educational map, he first gave a firm, organized form to Krishna Chandra Ramchandra Inter College, Shukulbazar, and so set educational development in motion.

Inspired by his revered mother, Smt. Chhiviraji Devi, he resolved to establish a college for higher education in Jagdishpur. To realize this vision, he founded the Sarvodaya Sewa Sansthan, Jagdishpur, and with the support of his associates named the college after Shri Rajiv Gandhi. Teaching began in 1998 on a self-financed basis, affiliated to Dr. Ram Manohar Lohia Awadh University, Ayodhya.

Milestones in Our Growth

Key historical events that shaped Rajiv Gandhi Mahavidyalaya into an educational beacon.

Our Founder

Pandit Shyam Lal Tiwari

Founder, Rajiv Gandhi Mahavidyalaya (1963 – 2002)

Pandit Shyam Lal Tiwari (born 1963) was a socially aware citizen, reformer, and thinker who devoted his life to the upliftment of society and the nation. He worked actively alongside Smt. Indira Gandhi, India’s first woman Prime Minister, and later drew political inspiration from Shri Rajiv Gandhi.

Believing that education is the surest path to development, he established the college in the small town of Jagdishpur for students who had been denied access to higher education. After sustained efforts, the foundation stone was laid on Friday, 27 April 2001, by Smt. Sonia Gandhi, then Leader of the Opposition and MP from Amethi. The region thereby gained a respected institution that continues to serve society.

He earned a Master’s degree in Defence Studies from Lucknow University and was known as a forceful orator and keen political analyst. His contribution to education remains a lasting inspiration for the region.

पंडित श्याम लाल तिवारी (संस्थापक, राजीव गाँधी महाविद्यालय)

पंडित श्याम लाल तिवारी का जन्म सन 1963 में हुआ। समाज के जागरूक नागरिक, समाज सुधारक एवं चिंतक के रूप में उन्होंने सदैव समाज और राष्ट्र के उत्थान के लिए कार्य किया। आप देश को सुदृढ़ बनाने में भारत की प्रथम महिला प्रधानमंत्री श्रीमती इंदिरा गांधी जी के साथ सक्रिय रहे और आगे चलकर श्री राजीव गांधी जी आपके राजनीतिक संरक्षक बने।

अपने उन्नत विचारों एवं विकासोन्मुख सोच के साथ उन्होंने जगदीशपुर जैसे छोटे नगर में राजीव गाँधी महाविद्यालय की स्थापना की, ताकि शिक्षा से वंचित रह रहे विद्यार्थियों को उच्च शिक्षा का अवसर प्राप्त हो सके। शिक्षा की इस आवश्यकता को दूर करने के लिए उन्होंने निरंतर प्रयास किए और 27 अप्रैल 2001, दिन शुक्रवार को तत्कालीन नेता प्रतिपक्ष एवं अमेठी सांसद श्रीमती सोनिया गांधी जी के कर-कमलों द्वारा महाविद्यालय का शिलान्यास करवाकर इस क्षेत्र को एक प्रतिष्ठित शैक्षणिक संस्था प्रदान की। आपने लखनऊ विश्वविद्यालय से रक्षा अध्ययन में स्नातकोत्तर की उपाधि प्राप्त की।

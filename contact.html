Located on Shukul Bazar Road in Jagdishpur, District Amethi, Uttar Pradesh – 227809.

By Shukul Bazar Road

The college is situated right on Shukul Bazar Road in Jagdishpur, easily accessible from the town center and local market.

By Railway (Nihalgarh Station)

Nihalgarh Railway Station (NHH, Jagdishpur) is just 2.5 km from the college, with direct train connections to Lucknow, Varanasi, Sultanpur, and Delhi.

By Highway (NH-56 / NH-731)

Direct connectivity via the 4-lane Lucknow-Varanasi Highway. Regular UPSRTC buses link Jagdishpur with Lucknow (75 km) and Sultanpur (55 km).

Campus Parking

Spacious, secure vehicle parking available at the main gate for two-wheelers and four-wheelers.
